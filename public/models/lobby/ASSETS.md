## Changes

`sofa_module.glb`: one curved sofa module (seat and back cushions, back frame, throw pillow) with ambient
occlusion baked into vertex colours in Cycles. Dimensions come from `LOUNGE_ISLANDS`; the build checks that
the module stays inside its sector. The editable `.blend` is kept outside the repo in `assets/source/blender/`.

`pachira_aquatica_01.glb` is built by `scripts/build-lobby-tree.mjs` from the source kept outside the repo in
`assets/source/polyhaven/pachira_aquatica_01/` (not committed):

- kept only variant d (`pachira_aquatica_01_bark_d`, `pachira_aquatica_01_leaves_d`), moved to the origin;
- simplified to ~50 % (meshoptimizer), textures resized to 512² WebP, meshopt geometry compression.
